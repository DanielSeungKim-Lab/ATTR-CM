DATA-01-trial-race-counts.csv   one row per trial
  trial, nct_id                  trial name and ClinicalTrials.gov identifier
  black ... other_or_not_reported  participants in each reported race category
  race_reported_total            sum of the race categories, as reported
  enrollment_randomized          randomized participants; exceeds race_reported_total where
                                 a participant has no race record or was not dosed
  race_source                    where the counts were read

DATA-02-benchmark-inputs.csv   one row per quantity
  key                            identifier used by the scripts
  value, unit                    the quantity and what it counts
  source                         the publication or data product it was read from

DATA-03-site-counties.csv   one row per trial and county
  nct_id, trial                  the trial whose sites fall in this county
  county_fips, county_name       5-digit FIPS code and name
  n_sites                        that trial's sites in this county
  black_alone_75plus,
  white_alone_75plus             county population aged 75 and over
  A county hosting sites in more than one trial appears once per trial.

DATA-04-landmark-disposition.csv   one row per landmark trial
  nct_id, acronym, phase, enrollment, pheno   registry fields; pheno is CM for a
                                 cardiomyopathy population and mixed where polyneuropathy
                                 is also enrolled
  tier                           A pivotal, B1 randomized phase 2, B2 open-label extension
  race_reported                  whether race was retrievable
  disposition                    how the trial was used
